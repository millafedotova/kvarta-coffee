# Image generation prompts

Mode: built-in image generation. Product images are new fictional packaging, followed by precise edits to remove invented information. The workshop scene is a generated illustration of the process. Output files are listed in [assets](assets.md).

## Product generation

The three origin prompts used the same structure, substituting origin and accent:

> A single 250 g matte warm-white stand-up coffee pouch, crisp natural paper texture, large bold deep forest green Cyrillic wordmark “КВАРТА” on a clean front label, small origin label and “250 г”. A muted sage (Ethiopia), warm clay (Colombia), or golden ochre (Brazil) rectangular block distinguishes the origin. One pouch alone, centered and fully visible, upright, subtle three-quarter angle, on a solid warm off-white studio background #eeeee7 with a short natural shadow. Confident contemporary graphic design, simple small-roastery packaging, no glossy luxury. Square editorial product photograph. No extra products, props, plants, floating beans, or watermarks. Product occupies about 75% of the height. Brand spelling must be exactly К В А Р Т А.

## Final label correction

> Edit this product photo precisely. Preserve the pouch, perspective, background, natural lighting, material texture, and current colored label block. Replace all printed text with a minimal label system containing only: large “КВАРТА”; small “обжарка · Минск”; the exact origin, exact taste notes, and “250 г”. Remove every other slogan, date, growing/processing/roast claim, and decorative illustration. There must be no 2018, no Москва, and no invented information. Keep a clean typographic layout with breathing room. Keep the sage/clay/ochre color unchanged. Product photography remains photorealistic and otherwise unchanged.

Exact origin/taste pairs:

- “ЭФИОПИЯ ИРГАЧЕФФЕ” / “цветочный · бергамот”.
- “КОЛУМБИЯ УИЛА” / “карамель · красное яблоко”.
- “БРАЗИЛИЯ СЕРРАДО” / “орех · шоколад · под эспрессо”.
